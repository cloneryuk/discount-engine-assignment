How pricing works
  • Best non-stackable discount (largest rupee saving) is picked when multiple match.
  • Stackable offers apply on top of the winner, in rule-ID order.
  • Cart-level offers are applied on the discounted subtotal; threshold must be met.
  • Items with no matching rule keep their base price.
